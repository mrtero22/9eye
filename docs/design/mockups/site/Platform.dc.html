Traffic and scheduling tools for the people who run the channel.

Schedulers, traffic managers and playout operators work on one plan, each with the tools for their part of it.

Frame-accurate time

Every time is kept in frames at the channel's own rate, from 23.976 to 59.94, drop-frame timecode included.

Any region, any week

Broadcast day start, first weekday and time zone are set per channel, so each feed runs on local time.

Programmes and breaks together

A main playlist and an ads playlist, linked at exact break positions, so changes in one show up in the other.

Series that run themselves

The next episode in the next slot, repeats days later, and every airing shifted at once when plans change.

Ads, promos and fillers

Commercials and promos placed with clash codes kept apart; rotation lists keep idents and fillers fresh.

Checked before air

Gaps, overlaps, missing media and expired rights are flagged before the playlist is exported.

Your MAM, your playout

Adapters connect the media asset management and playout automation you already run, without locking you in.

Many channels, many teams

Scheduling software for every kind of TV channel.

Linear broadcast, FAST and streaming channels share one core. Series, film, music, news, sport, kids and public channels differ only in settings and rules — never a separate product.

  • Series and entertainment

    Episodes split at break points, repeats and sister channels.

  • Film

    Long blocks, with break points taken from each title's segments.

  • Music and clips

    Rotation lists that keep artists and categories apart.

  • News

    Fixed bulletins and last-minute changes without losing the day.

  • Sport and live

    Live events and overruns, with the rest of the schedule kept in step.

  • Kids

    Air windows keep every item in the hours it may be shown.

  • FAST and streaming

    Always-on channels with programme guides for streaming platforms.

  • Public media

    Schedules with little or no advertising, on the same core.
