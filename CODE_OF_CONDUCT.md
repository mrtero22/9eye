# Code of Conduct

9eye is built by and for people who keep channels on air. We want taking part to be welcoming and
useful for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Explain the broadcaster's need behind a request; listen to other channels' needs too.
- Respect people's time: clear issues, small pull requests, honest answers.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing a broadcaster's confidential data (schedules, clients, contracts) in issues or examples —
  use made-up names.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break this code. Report a
problem to **studio@9eye.org**; reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and every other 9eye community space.
